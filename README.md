my personal nix config files for desktops and users alike.

## what's here

### host `deaddove`

my main pc, a ryzen 7 2700 with 24gb (3x8GB) ddr4 and multiple terabytes of
storage. running various tailnet-enabled services. i promise i'll get her a
nice big upgrade soon enough, she deserves it after everything.

### host `villa`

my old laptop, a dell latitude 5421 with an i7 11850h and 24gb of ddr4. somehow
died of a board fault within two weeks of me actually getting it, it happens.
kept here for completeness, this laptop still holds a place in my heart.

### darwin host `natura`

a prospective buy; a freshly minted 32gb/1tb m6 macbook air. expecting to have
the money to make the investment sometime early 2027. hostname was decided far
in the past, a throwback to a former chosen name of someone who i hold very
close even still.

### user `aenri`

a simple cli-only subset of my configurations set up as a home-manager config.

### user `aenri@gui`

graphical desktop access but no nixos? worry not! for i have all of my config
files set up with this one, and a bunch of applications.
